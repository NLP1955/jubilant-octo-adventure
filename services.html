Service 1

Surplus Funds Recovery

When a property is sold at a tax deed auction or foreclosure sale for more than what was owed, the surplus belongs to the former owner. We find those funds, locate the rightful owners, and handle every step of the claim — at zero upfront cost to you.

How It Works:

  1. Research — We dig through county tax deed and foreclosure records to identify surplus funds and the rightful owners.
  2. Skip Tracing — We locate former property owners or their heirs, no matter where they've moved.
  3. Direct Mail Notice — We send a formal letter explaining the funds and how to claim them.
  4. Phone Follow-Up — We call to walk you through the process and answer all questions.
  5. Attorney Opens the Claim — We work with licensed attorneys to prepare and file the claim with no cost to you upfront.
  6. Mobile Notary — A notary comes to you — home, office, hospital, anywhere — to sign documents properly.
  7. Electronic Filing — We file all court documents electronically. Fast. Trackable. Secure.
  8. Court Monitoring — We monitor your claim and keep you informed every step of the way.
  9. You Receive Your Check — The county issues the check. You get paid. We get paid only then.
