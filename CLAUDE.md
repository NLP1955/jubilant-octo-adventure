### Pipeline: Surplus Funds Recovery
1. **Lead Identified** — Property owner found with potential surplus funds
2. **Contacted** — Initial outreach made (phone/email/mailer)
3. **Interested** — Owner responded and is interested
4. **Agreement Sent** — Authorization/fee agreement sent via Docusign
5. **Agreement Signed** — Contract executed
6. **Claim Filed** — Surplus fund claim submitted to county/court
7. **Funds Received** — Disbursement received
8. **Closed Won** — Fee collected, case complete
9. **Closed Lost** — Did not proceed

### Key Contact Properties to Track
- Property address
- County / State
- Surplus fund amount
- Claim deadline / filing deadline
- Source (skip trace, county record, tax sale list)

## Surplus Funds Research Process
1. Pull lists from county tax collector / clerk of court records
2. Identify properties sold at auction with surplus proceeds
3. Skip trace to locate current property owner / heir
4. Outreach via mailer, phone, email
5. Execute authorization agreement via Docusign
6. File claim with county/court
7. Track disbursement and collect fee
