Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is process serving?

Process serving is the formal delivery of legal documents — such as a summons, complaint, or subpoena — to a party involved in a court case, in a manner that satisfies state legal requirements.

How fast can you serve documents?

Standard service is typically completed within 5–7 business days. Rush and same-day service are available for time-sensitive matters.

What areas do you cover?

We serve documents statewide, including both metro and rural areas, through our network of licensed process servers.

What happens if the recipient avoids service or can't be found?

Our servers make multiple documented attempts at different times and locations. If needed, we offer skip tracing to help locate a recipient, and can advise on alternative service options where permitted by the court.

Do you provide proof of service?

Yes. Every completed serve comes with a notarized affidavit of service, formatted to meet court filing requirements.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on service speed and location. Contact us for a flat-rate quote — there are never hidden fees.
