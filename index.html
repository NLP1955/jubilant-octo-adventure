What Are Surplus Funds?

When a county sells a property to collect unpaid taxes — or a lender forecloses and auctions it off — the sale price often exceeds what was owed. That extra money is called a surplus, and it belongs to the former owner. We navigate the entire process so you collect what is rightfully yours.

🏠

1. Property Sold at Auction

A home is auctioned to satisfy a tax debt or mortgage. The winning bid is often far higher than what was owed.

💵

2. Surplus Is Created

The debt is paid from sale proceeds. The leftover amount — the surplus — legally belongs to the former owner.

🏛️

3. County Holds the Money

The surplus sits with the county clerk or court, unclaimed because most people never know it exists.

✅

4. NLP Sales Recovers It

We handle all the paperwork, navigate the legal process, and get your money returned to you. No recovery = no fee.
