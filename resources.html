Answers to the most common questions we hear from families across Florida.

Surplus funds (also called "overbid funds" or "excess proceeds") are the money left over after a property is sold at a tax deed auction or foreclosure sale for more than the amount owed on the debt. For example, if a home is sold at auction for $150,000 but the owner only owed $80,000 in back taxes, the $70,000 difference is the surplus — and it legally belongs to the former owner, not the government or the new buyer.
