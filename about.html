About NLP Sales LLC

A history of service. A commitment to the community.

Meet Nelson Perez

Founder & CEO, NLP Sales LLC | Former Hillsborough County Deputy Sheriff

Nelson Perez began his law enforcement career with the Washington D.C. Metropolitan Police Department from 1986 to 1987. He then returned to Florida, where he dedicated 27 years to the Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office (1987–2015), proudly serving the residents of our community and earning the respect of colleagues and citizens alike.

After retiring from public service, Nelson founded NLP Sales, LLC to help individuals and families identify and recover surplus funds that may still belong to them from tax deed sales, foreclosure auctions, and other public record sources.

"After decades of public service, we continue helping Florida families recover money that may rightfully belong to them. Our mission is simple: treat people with honesty, respect, and the same integrity we lived by in public service."
— Nelson Perez

Today, NLP Sales serves clients in Hillsborough, Hernando, Sarasota, Marion, Lee, and all surrounding Florida counties. Nelson's team includes experienced researchers, licensed attorneys, and a nationwide mobile notary network — all working together to make the recovery process simple, fast, and free for clients until they get paid.
