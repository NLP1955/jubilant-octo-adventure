"Once John and Mary decided to move forward, here's what happened:

**Step 1: Attorney opens the claim.**
We work with licensed Florida attorneys who specialize in surplus fund recovery. They review the case, prepare the motion, and file it with the circuit court.

**Step 2: Mobile notary comes to the family.**
John and Mary didn't have to go anywhere. Our mobile notary came to their daughter's home. All documents were signed in front of two witnesses — legally and properly.

**Step 3: Electronic filing with the court.**
We filed all documents electronically with the county court. Faster. Trackable. Secure. No paper delays.

**Step 4: The 2–3 month wait.**
Surplus fund claims typically take 2 to 3 months for the court to process and approve. This is normal. We kept John and Mary informed every step of the way — no surprises.

**Step 5: The county issues the check.**
